## 4. Notes on asset provenance

- Textures are taken from the upstream MIT repository, so they are covered by that MIT grant.
  If a texture is later found to originate from a third party that is not credited upstream,
  it must be replaced or credited here.
- `export/` contains analysis artifacts (item tables, rendered icons, upstream model copies).
  It is reference material for development and must **not** be shipped in a release.
- Ponder scene structures (`assets/greate_remake_remake/ponder/*.nbt`) are generated by
  `tools/gen_ponder_structures.py` from vanilla blocks plus this mod's blocks.
